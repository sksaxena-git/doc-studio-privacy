Doc Studio Pro Lifetime

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 September 2026

Applies to: Doc Studio Pro Lifetime for iOS and Android, version 0.2.0 and later versions with the same data practices

Publisher: DS-Builder

Privacy contact: contact@brightindians.com

Doc Studio Pro Lifetime (“Studio”, “we”, or “the app”) helps you scan documents, extract text, edit photos, create ID photos, compose PDFs, and compress files. The app is designed to process your documents and photos on your device. The current app does not require a Studio account, upload your content to a developer-operated service, show advertisements, or include developer-operated analytics or tracking.

Information the app uses

When you choose to take or import a photo or document, Studio accesses only the item you select through the camera, Photos, or Files interface. Processing can include image pixels, PDF pages, recognized text, document details such as names or ID numbers, and edits you make. This work is performed on the device. Studio does not send the selected content or extracted text to us for processing.

The app stores imported originals, saved results, edit information, names, and a local library index in its private app storage. It may also create temporary working files while processing or exporting. These files are not an online Studio account or cloud library. Your operating system may include app data in device backups, depending on your settings.

Studio uses camera and photo-library access only for features you initiate. It does not request your device location. An original photo can nevertheless contain location or other camera metadata already embedded in that file. Newly rendered image results are created without the original camera EXIF metadata; an unchanged original that you keep or share may retain it.

Exports and services you choose

You can save or share a result using your device's system interfaces. If you choose iCloud Drive, Google Drive, OneDrive, WhatsApp, or another destination, your device and the selected provider transfer and handle that copy under their own terms and privacy policies. Studio does not obtain your drive password or control copies after export.

Optional Studio Pro purchases and restores are handled by Apple App Store or Google Play. The app receives the product and transaction or entitlement status needed to unlock Pro features. Apple or Google handles payment details; we do not receive your complete payment-card number. Store transactions and account information are subject to the applicable store's privacy practices.

The current app contains no advertising SDK, cross-app tracking, developer-operated analytics, cloud OCR, or account-sync service. If we add any of these in a future release, we will update this policy and the relevant store privacy disclosures before that release.

Retention and deletion

Saved items remain in Studio's private local storage until you delete them or remove the app, subject to operating-system backup behavior. To delete a saved item and its app-retained originals, open Library, select the item, and choose Delete local project. Deleting from Studio does not delete copies you previously exported, files still held by the original source, or device backups. Manage those copies through the relevant app, provider, or device settings.

Studio does not operate an account or server-side document repository from which we can retrieve or delete your files. You can ask the publisher about this policy using the privacy contact above. Please do not email sensitive documents or identity photos when requesting support.

Security

Studio relies on the operating system's app storage isolation and permission controls. You control access to your unlocked device, backups, and destinations used for export. No digital storage or transfer method is completely risk-free, so review the destination and content before sharing sensitive documents.

Children

Studio is a general-purpose document utility, not a service directed to children. A parent or guardian should supervise a child's use of document capture, sharing, and purchases.

Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy when the app's practices change. The current version and effective date will appear at this public policy URL. Material changes will be reflected in the app or store listing when appropriate.

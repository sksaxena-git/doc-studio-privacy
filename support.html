Support

Publisher: DS-Builder

Support email: contact@brightindians.com

Typical response: within 3 business days

For help with Doc Studio Pro Lifetime, email contact@brightindians.com. Please include a short description of the issue, the app version, your device model, and the operating-system version.

When reporting a problem

Tell us which tool you were using (Scan, Extract Text, Enhance Photo, ID Photo, Compress, or PDF Composer), what you expected to happen, and what happened instead. If an export failed, include the file type and target size. Do not email Aadhaar numbers, PAN numbers, identity photos, or other sensitive documents; describe the issue without attaching personal documents.

Privacy and data

Doc Studio is designed to process your documents and photos on your device. Support does not require you to upload your files. Read the Privacy Policy for details about local storage, permissions, and sharing destinations you choose.

Purchases and Pro access

For a purchase or restore problem, include the store (Apple App Store or Google Play), the approximate purchase date, and the transaction or order reference with any sensitive payment details removed. DS-Builder cannot see your full payment-card number.
